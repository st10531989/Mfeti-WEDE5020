Welcome and about Mfeti Electrical Solution

Who are we?

Mfeti Elecrical Solutions is a small electrical services business located in Midrand, Gauteng. The business was started by the Mfeti family in 2024

The business provides electrical services to homes, business and other customers who require electrical solutions.

Our Mission

Is to provide safe, affordable and quality electrical services to homes and businesses in Gauteng, while also delivering reliable workmanship, excellent customer service and ensuring safety in every job.

Our Vision

Is to become one of the most tusted and leading electrical service providers in South Africa, to be recognized for professionalism, innovation and customer satisfaction.

Our Customers

  • Small businesses
  • Property managers
  • Schools
  • Churches
  • Other customers requiring electrical services
